The Ghost Who Started Everything
Satoshi
Nakamoto.

On October 31, 2008, an anonymous person or group using the name Satoshi Nakamoto published a 9-page whitepaper that would change the world. They built Bitcoin, launched it, corresponded with early developers for two years — then vanished. No one knows who they are. No one has ever moved a single coin from their estimated 1 million BTC wallet. The mystery remains unsolved.

"I've moved on to other things. It's in good hands with Gavin and everyone."
— Satoshi Nakamoto, last known message, April 23, 2011
Read the Whitepaper → Satoshi's Writings
The Whitepaper
Oct 31 2008
● Original Document
Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System
Satoshi Nakamoto — satoshin@gmx.com — bitcoin.org — October 31, 2008
A purely peer-to-peer version of electronic cash would allow online payments to be sent directly from one party to another without going through a financial institution. Digital signatures provide part of the solution, but the main benefits are lost if a trusted third party is still required to prevent double-spending. We propose a solution to the double-spending problem using a peer-to-peer network...
Download PDF → bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf
The Timeline
2008 — 2011
Oct 2008
The Whitepaper
Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System published
Posted to a cryptography mailing list on Halloween. Nine pages. No institution. No funding. Just an idea that would become a $1 trillion+ asset class.
Jan 3 2009
The Genesis Block
Block #0 mined. 50 BTC created. The network is born.
Embedded in the genesis block: "The Times 03/Jan/2009 Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks." A message. A timestamp. A manifesto.
Jan 12 2009
First Transaction
Satoshi sends 10 BTC to Hal Finney
The first peer-to-peer Bitcoin transaction. Hal Finney, a cryptographer and one of the earliest believers, receives the first ever Bitcoin transfer.
May 22 2010
Bitcoin Pizza Day
10,000 BTC exchanged for two pizzas
Laszlo Hanyecz makes the first real-world Bitcoin purchase. Those pizzas are now worth hundreds of millions of dollars. Every May 22nd, the world remembers.
Dec 2010
The Withdrawal
Satoshi begins stepping back from the project
Passes development control to Gavin Andresen. Becomes less active on forums. The handoff begins quietly.
Apr 23 2011
The Disappearance
Last known communication. Then silence.
A brief email to Gavin Andresen: "I've moved on to other things. It's in good hands." No further contact. No identity revealed. Gone forever — or perhaps watching.

Legislation Watch
U.S. Policy
● Pending Senate Vote
GENIUS Act — Stablecoin Regulation Framework
Would require stablecoin issuers to hold 1:1 reserves and register with federal regulators. Key vote expected this session. Direct impact on USDT, USDC, and DAI holders across the board.
● House Passed
FIT21 — Financial Innovation and Technology for the 21st Century Act
Establishes CFTC jurisdiction over digital commodities vs SEC for securities. The most important structural crypto bill in U.S. history. Awaiting Senate action.
● Legal Battle
IRS DeFi Broker Rule
Contested rulemaking requiring DeFi protocols to report user transactions to the IRS. Multiple legal challenges filed by crypto advocacy groups. Outcome will define DeFi privacy for years.
● Executive Order
Bitcoin Strategic Reserve
Treasury directed to explore a national Bitcoin reserve. Multiple states introducing companion legislation. Historic shift in U.S. sovereign Bitcoin policy — the world is watching.
● Active Litigation
SEC vs. Crypto — Ongoing Cases
Multiple enforcement actions still working through courts following Ripple precedent. Coinbase, Binance, and others fighting to define whether tokens are securities. Landmark rulings expected.
● Committee Review
Digital Asset Anti-Money Laundering Act
Would extend Bank Secrecy Act requirements to crypto wallets and miners. Heavily opposed by the crypto industry as unconstitutional. Watch this one closely.
